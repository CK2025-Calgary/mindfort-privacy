# Privacy Policy for MindFort
**Last Updated: July 20, 2025**

MindFort is a mood and stressor tracking app developed by CK2025-Calgary. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information Collection and Use
- MindFort stores mood and stressor data locally on your device using shared_preferences, a Flutter package.
- No personal information (e.g., name, email) is collected.
- No data is transmitted, shared, or sold to third parties.

## Data Storage
- All data is stored locally on your device and can be cleared within the app via the "Clear All Data" feature.
- We do not access, process, or store data on external servers.

## Security
- We use basic encryption provided by your device’s storage system, but this app is not designed for highly sensitive data.
- Users are responsible for securing their devices.

## Changes to This Policy
- We may update this policy as the app evolves. Check this page for the latest version.

## Contact Us
- For questions, email customercare@mindfort.net 

## Disclaimer
- MindFort is not a medical or mental health service. It is not a substitute for professional advice. Consult a healthcare provider for serious concerns.
